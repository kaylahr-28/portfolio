I am a film team member for RIT's NOVA Dance Crew, involved in planning, filming, and promoting the club.

The Process

As a fan of both dance and K-Pop, I joined NOVA Dance Crew to further pursue my interests. I help film bi-weekly workshops and create thumbnails to be posted on Youtube. Additionally, I help film and plan the logistics and promotion of 1-2 film covers per semester. Using Premiere Pro, I edit these film covers. I also use Photoshop and Illustrator to edit concept photos for film covers to be posted on the club's Instagram.
