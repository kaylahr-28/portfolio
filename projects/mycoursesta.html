This hi-fi prototype proposes an updated user flow of the MyCourses Teaching Assistant view.

The Process

As a Teaching Assistant during my sophomore year, I have experienced frustrations with the design and layout of MyCourses' TA view. Its inaccurate grouping of assignments on the Home page and overwhelming Submissions page made it difficult to effectively locate submissions I needed to grade. To see if this was a common issue, I collected data from other TAs on their experiences with MyCourses. They all shared similar anecdotes, finding a common ground in their issues with TA assignments being wrongly labelled as "Overdue". So, I sought to address this problem by improving the taxonomy of the Home page and simplifying the navigation of the Submissions page.

My initial sketch compares the current MyCourses view and my proposed view. I used this as a baseline for my Figma lo-fi prototype, focusing on separation of information and navigation to the appropriate pages. After receiving feedback from five student TAs, I expanded upon my lo-fi prototype to create a hi-fi version in Axure. I implemented a responsive filter feature on the Submissions page and a responsive rubric on the Feedback page to further simulate the experience of locating and grading an assignment.
